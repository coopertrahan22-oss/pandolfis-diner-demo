Our Story

One of the Last Real Diners on the South Coast

Pandolfi's Mattapoisett Diner has been an anchor of this community for over 50 years. Parked right on Route 6, it's the kind of place that was here when your parents were growing up, and it'll be here for your kids too.

This is a real streamline diner — the old-school kind. Fresh coffee comes in hefty white mugs and it never stops. Every plate is made to order, every morning, no exceptions. The home fries are crispy. The omelets are fat and golden. The linguica cheese rolls are legendary.

People come from all over the South Coast — some for the famous chili, some for the Benny's, some just for the rosemary home fries. Whatever brings you in, you'll leave with a full stomach and a reason to come back.
