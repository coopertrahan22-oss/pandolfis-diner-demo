A Mattapoisett Institution

The Diner Mattapoisett Counts On

Pandolfi's Diner has been a cornerstone of Mattapoisett's community for generations. From early-rising fishermen to families grabbing Sunday breakfast, we've always been the place where the coffee's hot, the food is real, and everyone knows your name.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen is made to order — no shortcuts, no frozen shortcuts. Just good ingredients, a hot griddle, and a team that cares about sending you out the door with a full stomach and a smile.

Walk-ins always welcome. Bring the kids, bring the crew, or just bring yourself. There's always a seat at Pandolfi's.
